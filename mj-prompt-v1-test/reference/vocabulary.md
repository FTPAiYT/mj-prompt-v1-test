# Vocabulary — words that move the image

A curated word bank for Midjourney prompting: the words a creator actually endorses for changing how an image **looks**, **feels**, and is **made**, plus the color and form tricks that force specific results. Distilled from a working note-vault and course notes (MJ v6/v7 era, ~2025).

**Legend**
- 🟢 **durable** — version-agnostic; the word/idea should keep working.
- 🟡 **volatile** — version- or model-tied; verify before leaning on it.
- Quotes in "..." are the creator's own verdict. Names in (parens) are community attributions.
- A `?` next to a word = the creator was unsure it works.

---

## 1. The one idea: words do the work of paragraphs

🟢 A prompt is more than *subject + setting*. You steer the look by stacking words from a few categories. **The exact category label doesn't matter** — knowing the options is what counts.

🟢 **Power word** = any single word that *overpowers and transforms a whole generation* — it "does the work of entire paragraphs." Drop one in and the image lurches away from MJ's default.

🟢 **Even an imperfect hit still helps:** *"certain words WILL generate different styles… even if the AI doesn't nail the style completely, using the word will still alter the images away from the default."* So reach for rare, specific words to escape the generic stock look.

🟢 **The dictionary meaning doesn't matter** — you're discovering what *the model* associates with a word, not what Merriam-Webster says. Test, don't assume.

### Clean structure
🟢 **`[power word] style [subject]`** — e.g. `cybernetic style koala`, `elegant penguin in an elegant setting`.

🟢 **Test-a-word base prompt:** `Portrait of an Instagram model, ___ style` — drop any aesthetic/style word in the blank and compare against the default.

🟢 **All art styles are power words, but not all power words are art styles.**

### Placement
🟢 Whatever you write **first carries the most weight** (the "triangle": style / subject / background). `a spaceship with an anthropomorphic leopard pirate inside` ≠ the reverse. Put the concept you care about most up front.

### Combining
🟢 **Styles blend.** `chibi watercolor sportscar` ≈ a ~50/50 mix. Combining is the best part of working with AI.
🟢 **Don't fear opposites** — *"just because it's hard for us to imagine doesn't mean it's hard for the AI."* Mash contradictions: `retro-futuristic`, `rustic-smooth`, `Viking-Samurai`.
🟢 **Reverse-engineer your favorites:** run `/describe` on an image you built to see what your power words are actually made of.

## 2. Style vs Aesthetic vs Medium — how it LOOKS / FEELS / is MADE

🟢 Three overlapping lenses. The creator's own split:

| Lens | Question | The creator's gloss | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Style** | *How it looks* | "how the image looks" (cartoon = a style) | watercolor, chibi, pixel art, caricature, de stijl, rubber hose cartoon |
| **Aesthetic** | *How it feels / what's in it* | "kinda what's in the image" (a cabin = an aesthetic) | cottagecore, emo, gothic, film noir, hellenic, medieval, hippie, cyberpunk |
| **Medium** | *How it's made* | the physical material/technique | oil painting, origami, beadwork, collage, woodworking |

Labels bleed into each other — `art deco` is both a vibe and a look. Don't agonize; pick the word that gets the pixels you want.

- **Lovecraftian** 🟢 — "pretty cool"; cosmic-horror tentacles.

- **heightened reality** 🟢 — punchy hyper-real look (e.g. "heightened reality batman style").
- **mundane** 🟢 — deliberately ordinary, anti-epic.
- **stylized** 🟢 — the *word* itself, separate from the `--s` parameter.
- **Saudade** 🟢 — wistful, longing mood. (Leo)
- **ineffable design** 🟡 — pairs with "randomness overdose, chromatic, chaos/chaotic." (Aritra Mukherjee)

### Use with care
- **cursed look** 🟡 — the eerie glitched vibe. NOTE: the famous `--style cursed` is a **parameter** from the v3/v4 era (volatile, may not exist in your version) — don't rely on the flag; the *vibe word* is the durable takeaway.
- **maximum texture** 🟢 — maximalist detail booster; lives in the popular T-shirt formula `surreal fantasy [subject] abstract in infographic style without text, maximum texture --no text`. (Goofy Sloth)

### Known DUDS (don't bother)
- **Autostereogram / "magic eye picture"** 🟢 — "kind of works" at best; plain `Autostereogram` → *"no lol it does not work."*
- **stereogram?** 🟡 — flagged as a word that may not produce the intended effect.
- General warning the creator gives: some words are *too* strong (they hijack everything) and some simply don't do what you'd expect. Add them one at a time to learn which is which.

## 7. Color language

🟢 **tint / tone / shade / hue are ~synonymous** — no big practical difference in MJ. Lean on **tone** and **tint**. (Treat `tint` as "might work.")

🟢 **Force a color with a hyphen — `[color]-colored`** (e.g. `mint-colored`, `x-colored`). The hyphen makes MJ commit to the color.

🟢 **Monochrome + one color.** Bare `monochrome` is unreliable — *you must name the color*: `monochrome red`, or describe it (`red roses`), or `[color] monotone` (e.g. `black and fuschia`).

🟢 **"mostly [color]"** — "a fun way to" name the dominant color; works well with `--raw`.

🟢 **ombre** — blends *between* colors (e.g. `blue / green / yellow ombre`).

🟢 **Describe palettes in plain language** — `palette of cool purples and blues` "works great." Plain warm/cool/family descriptions beat theory jargon.

🟢 **Add `reflection` when your prompt has a color glow** — reinforces the luminous/wet look. (Also `reflection in a lens / water / sunglasses / mirror` as a compositional move.)

🟢 Other handy color words: **desaturated** ("a cool word") · **high contrast** · **vibrant** · `by lisa frank` (instant saturated unicorn/rainbow colors).

### Color theory: what actually works (tested by the creator)
| Term | Verdict |
|---|---|
| `monochrome` + named color | ✅ works |
| plain-language palette ("cool purples and blues") | ✅ "works great" |
| `tint` | 🟡 might work |
| `triadic` | 🟡 maybe |
| `viridis` | 🟡 "kinda works but strange" |
| `complementary` | ❌ no |
| `analogous` | ❌ no ("but still cool") |
| `split-complementary` | ❌ no |
| `tetradic` | ❌ no |
| **hex codes** | ❌ **do NOT work** |
| **RGB codes** | ❌ **do NOT work** |
| `colorblocking` | ❌ don't phrase it that way — describe the palette plainly instead |

🟢 **Gotcha — runaway rainbow:** some words (e.g. `unicorn`) default to rainbow. Cancel it by stating the color you actually want.

## 8. Form & modifier words (shape, scale, configuration)

These bend the *form* of the subject without describing a whole new scene.

**Shape it:**
- **`-shaped`** 🟢 — `star-shaped logo`; `fireworks shaped like an eagle`. (Order matters: "fireworks in the shape of an eagle" = fireworks first, eagle as the silhouette.)
- **`-inspired`** 🟢 — `hulk koala` / `hulk-inspired koala`; borrow a flavor without a full crossover.
- **`amorphic`** 🟢 — a "power suffix" to **combine two things** / blob them into one form.
- **`made of ___`** 🟢 — `a human silhouette made of different algorithms`, `x-shape made of x` (fractal effect). Name the substance.
- **`arranged in [shape]` / `enclosed in a circle`** 🟢 — lay objects out in a form.

**Anthropomorphize:**
- **`anthropomorphic`** 🟢 — the single best word for human-like features on anything. Synonyms: **bipedal**, **anthropoid**.
- **`avian velociraptor`** 🟢 — pattern for "reptile with feathers"; describe the hybrid you want.

**Scale & manner adjectives** 🟢 — change the scene by emphasizing the *how*: `gigantic` vs `miniature`, `colossal`, `oversized`, plus atmosphere words for mood.

**Relational / state words** 🟢:
- **`engulfed in` / `engulfed by`** — swallow the subject in something.
- **`overlapping`** — layered, interleaved forms.
- **`superimposed`** — best for laying **titles / text** over an image.
- **`reinterpreted` / `decontextualized` / `reimagined`** 🟡 — push a familiar thing somewhere strange (creator was a little unsure of the first two).

**Phrasing wins** 🟢:
- **`armored X` beats `X wearing armor`** — fuse the trait into the noun.
- **`appropriate attire` / `uniform`** — "funny but works" to get sensible, on-theme clothing.

## Quick-reference cheat sheet

- **Structure:** `[power word] style [subject]` · test with `Portrait of an Instagram model, ___ style`.
- **Most important word goes first.**
- **Blend** styles and even opposites; run `/describe` to reverse-engineer a look.
- **Force color:** `[color]-colored`, `monochrome [color]`, `mostly [color]`, plain-language palettes. Hex/RGB and theory words (complementary/analogous/tetradic) **don't work**.
- **Force form:** `-shaped`, `made of ___`, `amorphic`, `anthropomorphic`.
- **Bankers:** cybernetic · elegant · surreal · birefringence · zentangle · low poly · illuminated manuscript · naive art · art brut · art toy.
- **Skip:** Autostereogram/magic-eye, the rejected `-core` list, color-theory jargon, hex/RGB.
- 🟡 Treat anything model/version-tied (cursed param, niji, lenticular, anaglyph) as "verify first."
